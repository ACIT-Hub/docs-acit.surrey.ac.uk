# Introduction to Accelerated Compute and the RIE role
## Overview

This module introduces the foundations of accelerated computing for the Research Infrastructure Engineering role.

???+ note "Who this module is for"
    This module is for junior Research Infrastructure Engineers (RIEs) and sysadmins who expect to work with HPC and accelerated compute. You might be:

    - New in a research computing or HPC support role. 
    - Moving from general IT/sysadmin work into research infrastructure. 
    - Curious about what RIEs actually do and what technologies you’ll be working with. 


???+ tip "Learning outcomes"
    By the end of this module, you will be able to:

    - Describe what high-performance computing (HPC) is and explain what GPUs are used for. 
    - Discuss the role of a Research Infrastructure Engineer and how it differs from other sysadmin roles. 
    - Outline some common tasks undertaken by junior RIEs related to accelerated compute. 
    - Discuss key ethics and sustainability considerations relevant to RIE work
